# Financial Rules

## Margin Rules

Always calculate:

Profit Margin % =
(Net Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100

---

## Expense Alerts

If:
Expenses > 70% of Revenue

Then:
- warn user
- suggest optimization

---

## Cash Runway Alerts

If:
Cash runway < 30 days

Then:
- alert user immediately
- recommend action plan

---

## Trend Analysis

Always compare with:
- previous month

- previous available data

Highlight:
- unusual growth

- declining margins
